Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Klazz ("we," "our," or "us") operates the website klazz2.netlify.app (and upcoming custom domains). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you visit our waitlist landing page.

1. Information We Collect

During our waitlist phase, we keep data collection to the absolute minimum:

  • Waitlist & Form Data: When you submit our form, we collect your email address and your chosen preference (joining the waitlist or applying as a campus ambassador) via Formspree.
  • Automatic Data: We do not use tracking cookies, and we do not log individual IP addresses on our servers. Basic standard web traffic logs may be handled securely by our host (Netlify) solely for operational uptime and security.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the email address you provide exclusively to:

  • Send you launch announcements, updates, and early access invitations for Klazz.
  • Review and respond to campus ambassador applications.

We will never sell, rent, or trade your email address to third parties.

3. Third-Party Services

We use Formspree to process our waitlist form submissions securely. Formspree handles submissions in compliance with privacy standards. You can review Formspree's privacy policy on their official website.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable precautions to protect your email address. However, no internet transmission is 100% secure.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Klazz grows and launches new features. Any updates will be posted on this page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out to us at: klazz.edu@gmail.com.
