The Problem

Nigerian students deserve better tools.

Academic Challenges

Past questions, lecture notes, and study guides are scattered across WhatsApp groups with no structure or reliability.

Fragmented Communities

Students struggle to connect across campuses, collaborate meaningfully, or safely buy and sell goods without a trusted platform.

Wellbeing & Navigation

Mental health support is scarce, campus navigation is manual, and student discounts are impossible to find in one place.

Our Solution

Three pillars. One platform.

Academic Solutions

  • Centralized hub for PDFs, notes & past questions
  • AI-powered study assistant & homework support
  • Mentorship & academic coaching connect
  • Personalized study schedule guidance

Community & Campus Life

  • Campus discussion forums across universities
  • Verified student marketplace (buy & sell)
  • In-app payments & QR verification
  • Peer connections and group collaboration

Wellbeing & Navigation

  • 24/7 mental health counseling access
  • Interactive campus map & facility finder
  • QR-based student discounts
  • Airtime & data purchase in-app

How It Works

From scattered groups to one campus home.

1

Choose your institution

Students select their university, polytechnic, or college to enter a campus-specific experience.

2

Join your campus space

Each campus gets organized resources, discussions, verified sellers, maps, and student updates.

3

Study, connect, and get support

Students can download materials, ask the AI assistant, meet mentors, trade safely, and access wellbeing help.
