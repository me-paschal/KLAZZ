About Klazz

Welcome to Klazz the digital operating system built specifically to transform the tertiary student experience across Nigeria.

Our Mission: Solving everyday student needs through technology, connection, and innovation.

The Story Behind Klazz

Every Nigerian student knows the struggle: hunting for past questions buried deep inside endless WhatsApp group chats, trying to coordinate group assignments across scattered platforms, or navigating campus life with zero centralized guidance.

As students ourselves, we lived through these exact frustrations. We realized that tertiary education in Nigeria demands better tools. That is why we built Klazz to bring academic resources, campus communities, student marketplaces, and mental health support together into one unified ecosystem.

What We Are Building

Klazz isn't just an app; it’s a campus home. Starting from our initial rollout across universities in Eastern Nigeria and expanding nationwide, we are creating a seamless platform where students can:

  • Access organized past questions, lecture notes, and study guides instantly.
  • Leverage AI-driven study tools to navigate complex coursework.
  • Trade safely and build trusted connections within their campus community.
  • Find essential student services, mental health resources, and campus maps without hassle.

Built by Students who understand school struggle

We understand the Nigerian student experience firsthand because we are right there in the lecture halls with you. Our team is driven by a passion to build homegrown technological solutions that make student life simpler, smarter, and more connected.
